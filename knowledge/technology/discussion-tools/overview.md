# Discussion Tools Comparison Matrix

## Overview

Comparison of five discussion platforms across six evaluation criteria for
RunicEngines' team collaboration needs. Scores are 1–10 (10 = best).

## Comparison Matrix

| Criteria | Discord | Mattermost | Slack | Zulip | Matrix/Element |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MCP Integration | 8 | 9 | 9 | 6 | 5 |
| Hosting | 3 | 9 | 2 | 8 | 10 |
| Cost | 9 | 7 | 5 | 9 | 8 |
| Features | 9 | 7 | 8 | 8 | 7 |
| Agent-Friendliness | 7 | 9 | 8 | 8 | 6 |
| Privacy | 4 | 9 | 4 | 8 | 10 |

## Platform Details

| Tool | Best For | Biggest Weakness |
|---|---|---|
| **Discord** | Rich communication (voice/video), community engagement | No self-hosting, weak privacy |
| **Mattermost** | Self-hosted, agent-friendly, full data control | Smaller ecosystem, no native voice/video |
| **Slack** | AI integration maturity, partner ecosystem | High cost at scale, SaaS-only |
| **Zulip** | Threaded async collaboration, value pricing | No official MCP server, smaller community |
| **Matrix/Element** | Privacy/sovereignty, federation, encryption | MCP ecosystem immature, ops complexity |
